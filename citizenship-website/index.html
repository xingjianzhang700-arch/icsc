About the Club

Interlake Citizenship Services Club (ICSC) is a student-run program that pairs aspiring US citizens with trained high school tutors. We provide friendly, patient support in the English language skills essential for passing the citizenship interview — completely free of charge.

What We Cover

📘

Civics

Practice all 100 USCIS civics questions on US history and government.

📖

Reading

Read the official USCIS vocabulary in clear, confident sentences.

✍️

Writing

Master sentence dictation from the USCIS writing word list.

🗣️

Speaking

Build comfort answering interview questions out loud with a real person.
